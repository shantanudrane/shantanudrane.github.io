I conduct fundamental and applied research in cryptography, security, privacy, machine learning and AI. Most of my recent work was funded through grants from DARPA and other US government agencies.

I have a Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu) and a Masters degree from [The University of Minnesota, Minneapolis](https://www.umn.edu), both in electrical engineering. I attended college in India, where I received my undergraduate degree in Instrumentation and Control Engineering at [Pune University](https://beta.unipune.ac.in).

I have previously worked at the [National Center for Radio Astrophysics](https://www.ncra.tifr.res.in) (Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Pune); Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories ([MERL](http://www.merl.com)) in Cambridge, MA; [Xerox PARC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PARC_(company)) in Palo Alto, CA; and [SRI International](https://www.sri.com) in Menlo Park, CA.
